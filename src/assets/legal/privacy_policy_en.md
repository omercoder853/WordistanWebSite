# Wordistan Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: October 29, 2026*

---

### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how **Wordistan** ("the Platform", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects personal information when you use our website and mobile application.

Wordistan is operated by **Ömer Faruk Gülşen** as an individual developer and data controller. We take the privacy and confidentiality of your personal information seriously.

This Policy describes our general data protection practices. Users located in the Republic of Türkiye should also consult the **Wordistan KVKK Privacy Notice** for comprehensive disclosures under Turkish Data Protection Law No. 6698.

---

### 2. Information We Process

#### 2.1. Account Information
- Email address and secure password (or full name and email received via Google OAuth).
- This information is processed to register your account, manage secure authentication sessions, and verify user credentials.
- When you authenticate via Google, your Google password is never requested or stored on Wordistan systems.

#### 2.2. Profile Information
- Date of birth and gender.
- These fields are processed in accordance with data minimization principles to manage user profiles, deliver age-appropriate learning experiences, and generate aggregated, anonymous demographic statistics.

#### 2.3. Learning Data and User Content
- Custom dictionaries, saved vocabulary entries, collections, personal notes, and contextual example sentences.
- Quiz and exercise performance, review cycles, learning milestones, and accuracy statistics.
- This data is processed to deliver core language-learning features, store user content, and maintain real-time synchronization across authenticated devices.

#### 2.4. Contact Form Information
- Full name, email address, and message contents submitted through the Wordistan contact form.
- This information is securely transmitted via the **Formspree** delivery infrastructure solely to review inquiries, provide technical support, and respond to your messages.
- Users are advised not to include sensitive personal details (such as identification numbers or payment data) in the contact form.

#### 2.5. Technical Information and System Logs
- The Wordistan website does not utilize third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts.
- Infrastructure and cloud partners (such as Supabase and hosting providers) may automatically process standard system access records (IP address, timestamps, etc.) strictly to protect platform security, mitigate cyber risks, and maintain uptime.

---

### 3. Guest Mode
You can use Wordistan in Guest Mode without registering an account.
- In Guest Mode, all created word lists, dictionaries, and exercise records are stored solely in local device storage.
- No Guest Mode data is transmitted to or stored on Wordistan's cloud databases.
- Deleting the app or resetting your device will permanently remove local data.

---

### 4. Purposes of Data Processing
Your personal data is processed for the following specified purposes:
- Creating, verifying, and managing user accounts;
- Providing educational features (dictionaries, vocabulary games, quizzes);
- Synchronizing data seamlessly across your devices;
- Addressing customer support requests and answering inquiries;
- Ensuring data security, network stability, and fraud prevention;
- Fulfilling mandatory statutory obligations under applicable law.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data with third parties for commercial advertising.

---

### 5. Third-Party Service Providers
Wordistan partners with reputable infrastructure providers to deliver reliable and secure operations:
- **Supabase:** Authentication services, secure PostgreSQL database management, and cloud storage.
- **Google:** OAuth authentication services when users select the "Sign in with Google" option.
- **Formspree:** Secure handling and delivery of messages submitted via the website contact form.

Wordistan does not sell your personal data to data brokers or advertising networks. The current beta version does not display third-party advertisements or offer in-app purchases.

---

### 6. International Data Transfers
Wordistan’s **Supabase** cloud infrastructure is hosted in Singapore. Accordingly, stored database records and profile data of registered users are securely hosted in Singapore.

International transfers are conducted in accordance with applicable data protection laws, utilizing strict encryption standards and security safeguards to guarantee high-level data protection.

---

### 7. Data Retention and Deletion
- **Active Accounts:** Personal data is retained for as long as your account remains active and necessary to provide the Service.
- **Account Deletion:** Users may delete their account at any time using the in-app "Delete Account" option. Account deletion immediately purges profile information and dictionaries from production databases.
- **Statutory Retention:** Specific records may be retained where required by law or for resolving legal disputes.
- **Technical Backups:** Disaster-recovery system backups are incrementally and securely overwritten within standard operational cycles of 30 to 90 days.

---

### 8. Data Security
We employ industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect personal data:
- TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit;
- Granular, role-based access control and encrypted database storage;
- Cryptographic one-way hashing for passwords.

Please note that no electronic transmission over the internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% immune from security vulnerabilities.

---

### 9. Your Rights as a Data Subject
Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:
- Inquire whether your personal data is being processed;
- Request access to and copies of your processed data;
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
- Request erasure or deletion of your personal data;
- Object to certain types of processing where applicable.

---

### 10. Children's Privacy
Wordistan does not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13 without appropriate parental or legal guardian consent. If you believe a minor has registered without such consent, please contact us immediately.
