### 4. Legal Grounds for Data Processing
Your personal data is processed under the statutory legal grounds set forth in Article 5 of Law No. 6698:

1. **Establishment or Performance of a Contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c):**  
   Directly necessary for creating user accounts, managing login sessions, providing learning services, and syncing dictionary data.
2. **Compliance with Legal Obligations (KVKK Art. 5/2-ç):**  
   Mandatory for complying with statutory record retention and responding to lawful requests from regulatory and judicial authorities.
3. **Legitimate Interests of the Data Controller (KVKK Art. 5/2-f):**  
   Necessary for cybersecurity management, bug detection, infrastructure optimization, and overall quality enhancement, provided that fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed.
4. **Explicit Consent (KVKK Art. 5/1):**  
   Applied where statutory exemptions do not exist, notably for international data storage and voluntary demographic profiling.

### 7. Data Retention and Erasure
- Personal data is retained throughout the active life cycle of your account and for the duration necessary to deliver the Service.
- When an account is deleted, all related personal records and dictionaries are promptly deleted from primary production databases.
- Where statutory retention obligations exist, data is maintained securely until statutory periods expire.
- Backup snapshots kept for disaster recovery are permanently purged or anonymized within periodic system cycles of 30 to 90 days.

---

### 8. Rights of the Data Subject (KVKK Article 11)
Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you are entitled to:
- Learn whether your personal data is being processed;
- Request information if your personal data has been processed;
- Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in accordance with its purpose;
- Know the third parties to whom personal data is transferred domestically or abroad;
- Request rectification of incomplete or inaccurate personal data;
- Request erasure or destruction of personal data under statutory conditions;
- Request notification of rectification, erasure, or destruction to third parties who received the data;
- Object to adverse outcomes resulting exclusively from automated analysis;
- Claim compensation for damages incurred due to unlawful processing.
